What is a Transformation?

A transformation is a rule that moves every point of a figure to a new position.

The given figure is the object; the figure we get is its image.

Naming

Object P(3, 4)  ⟶  Image P′(−4, 3)

Every point of the image takes a prime mark: A → A′, B → B″.

Two things to watch

  • Distance — does every length stay the same?
  • Direction — does the figure keep facing the same way?

Translation, reflection and rotation keep every length and angle: the image is congruent to the object.
